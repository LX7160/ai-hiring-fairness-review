# Algorithmic Bias, Mitigation, and Performance in AI-Based Hiring Systems: A Systematic Review

This review explored demographic bias, bias mitigation, and system performance in AI-based recruitment systems. It was completed as a final project for WRIT 109ST at UC Santa Barbara in the 2026 summer. 

## Research Questions (RQs)
- RQ1: What kinds of demographic biases might AI-based hiring systems exhibit?
- RQ2: How effective are algorithmic bias-mitigation methods at reducing these demographic biases in systems?
- RQ3: What effects do these algorithmic bias-mitigation methods have on system performance?

## Scope
The review includes 10 studies published in the ACM Digital Library and IEEE Xplore between 2020 and 2026. Datasets, models, fairness metrics, mitigation methods, and performance are compared and analyzed.

## Main Findings
- Gender and Race were demographic attributes that were studied most frequently.
- Different studies have employed different models, mitigation strategies, and evaluation methods, which limits the ability to make direct comparisons across studies regarding bias mitigation.
- The effectiveness of mitigation depends on the method used, model examined, demographic groups of interest, and evaluation metric used.
- Comparisons of model performance before and after implementing mitigation are limited, making it difficult to assess the relationship between fairness and performance.
